# Company Overview

## About SolarTech
SolarTech is a renewable energy company specializing in the design, manufacturing, and distribution of solar energy products.

## Mission
To accelerate the adoption of clean energy by delivering reliable, efficient, and affordable solar solutions.

## Core Values
- Sustainability
- Innovation
- Customer-centric design
- Quality and reliability

## Target Markets
- Residential solar installations
- Commercial and industrial solar systems
- Utility-scale solar projects
