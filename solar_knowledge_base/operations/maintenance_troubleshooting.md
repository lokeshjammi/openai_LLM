# Maintenance and Troubleshooting

## Routine Maintenance
- Clean panels every 3–6 months
- Inspect wiring annually
- Check inverter logs regularly

## Common Issues

### Low Power Output
- Possible Causes: Dust, shading, faulty connections
- Resolution: Clean panels, inspect wiring

### Inverter Shutdown
- Possible Causes: Grid fault, overheating
- Resolution: Check error codes, ensure ventilation

## Performance Monitoring
Use the SolarTech monitoring app to track daily and lifetime energy generation.
